Static lightmap baker

Developer tool. This is an offline diffuse GI bake of the fixed architectural proxy, not a per-visitor runtime renderer. It ray-traces sampled wall, floor and ceiling illumination and exports the baked RGB565 atlases as a JavaScript asset. Visitor-made art and movable display cases are not baked.

Ready to bake. The live museum reads static-lightmaps-data.js.
